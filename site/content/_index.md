# Welcome to 5th Rotherham

We're one of the fastest-growing Scout groups in the Rotherham District. Every week, young people aged 4-14 join our adventure right here in Maltby.

With the Mavericks, your child will be:

- **Making** new friends in a safe environment
- **Learning** awesome new skills for life
- **Having** unforgettable fun and adventures
- **Creating** memories that last a lifetime

Our thriving group is run by a dedicated team of trained volunteers who deliver an exciting programme, teaching valuable life skills while having plenty of fun along the way. Our adventures include regular meetings, camping trips, day activities, and playing an active part in the Maltby community.
