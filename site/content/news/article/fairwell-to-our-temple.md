Today is the end of an era, as the Temple (the old Fisher Building) has been demolished.

Over the years, the Temple has been used as an activity room, for storage, and it was also a wonderful place to hang out the tents to dry following a camp!

While it may have looked old and rickety (because it was!) – we will miss the Temple, and for those within Maltby that grew up in Scouting, we hope you have as fond a memory of the temple as we do.
