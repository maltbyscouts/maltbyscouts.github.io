On Saturday, 26th March 2022, members of 5th Rotherham (Maltby) Scout group went along with Maltby Town Council to help litter pick at Roche Abbey.

We couldn’t have asked for better weather, and the members from our Squirrels, Beavers, Cubs, and Scout section attended. We were even joined by Richard Hinchliffe, the District Commissioner for Rotherham Scouting.

Thank you to Tesco in Maltby for providing our young people with drinks and snacks after a hard slog litter-picking.

Also thank you to Maltby Town Council and the English Heritage for hosting the event.
