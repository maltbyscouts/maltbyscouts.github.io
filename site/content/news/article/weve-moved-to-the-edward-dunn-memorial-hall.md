After several months of planning, we’ve successfully moved to the Edward Dunn Memorial Hall!

A whole team of people helped us move our gear from the Coleman Centre over a total of sixteen hours.

We’d like to say a massive thank you to all the young people, parents and leaders that helped us move on the day, but also a thank you to [Maltby Town Council](https://www.maltbytowncouncil.co.uk/) for both supporting us on, and leading up to the day, as well as **RJ Construction Services Ltd in Maltby** who, without them, it wouldn’t have been possible.

We can’t wait to start back up again in September in our new home.

We’ll post an update with the new day and times soon so look for out for that.
